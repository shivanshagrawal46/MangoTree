# Sprint 9 — The Dashboard, Morning Briefing, Saved Reports

**Objective**: The home screen answers one question at a glance — **what needs a human, and what's handled** — and every user starts the day already briefed.

## Work Items

### The Pending-vs-Done Dashboard (UX-first)
- [ ] **Needs Attention column**: decisions waiting · JP Sir's review queue with aging · overdue commitments · critical findings · unread severity-4+ cards — each with **money-at-risk and a one-click action**
- [ ] **Handled by the System column**: auto-processed emails, auto-tracked commitments, auto-run analyses, drafts prepared — visible so the automation **earns trust by being seen**
- [ ] Per-user views: Rakesh Sir leads with decisions and money · JP Sir with his review queue · analyst with verification queues
- [ ] Portfolio grid: health, day counts, budget %, profit bands
- [ ] **Ad-hoc AI column**: ask a question → cited answer across every property (enumeration router underneath)

### Morning Briefing (per user, by 6 a.m.)
- [ ] Overnight intake · health deltas · tasks by money-at-risk with evidence links · deadline lookahead · overdue commitments with nudge drafts · significant cards
- [ ] Evidence-linked, action-paired, **ninety seconds to read, simple direct words**
- [ ] Registered as a standing scheduled job with SLA + dead-letter alert

### Saved Reports
- [ ] Any answer, brief, or evidence packet → formatted **PDF/Word with numbered references**
- [ ] Per-property and global **Reports Library with version history**
- [ ] **Scheduled recurring reports** (weekly portfolio, per-property status, JP Sir review digest) auto-generated and auto-emailed per the autonomy policy

## Gate
